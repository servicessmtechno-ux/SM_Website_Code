Warranty Policy

SM Techno Service stands behind the quality of our work. We provide a 90-day warranty on all repair services performed by our technicians.

Warranty Coverage

  • Duration: 90 days from the date of service completion.
  • Scope: The warranty covers the specific repair or parts replaced during the service.
  • Labor: Free service visits during the warranty period for the same issue.
  • Parts: Replacement parts used during repair are covered under the service warranty.

What is Covered

  • Recurring issues related to the original repair.
  • Defective parts replaced during the service.
  • Technical issues arising from the repair work performed.

What is NOT Covered

  • Issues unrelated to the original repair or service.
  • Damage caused by power surges, water damage, or physical damage.
  • Problems caused by unauthorized modifications or repairs by third parties.
  • Normal wear and tear of components not replaced during the service.
  • Issues caused by improper usage or lack of regular maintenance.

How to Claim Warranty

  1. Contact us via phone (+91 9908473572) or WhatsApp with your service details.
  2. Provide the service receipt or reference number from your original booking.
  3. Describe the issue you are experiencing.
  4. Our team will schedule a free follow-up visit to diagnose and resolve the issue.

Warranty is valid only for the original customer and is non-transferable. Please retain your service receipt for warranty claims.
